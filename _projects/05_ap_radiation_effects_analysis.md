Analyzed **space radiation environments** and evaluated **spacecraft shielding effectiveness** for LEO and MEO satellite missions using **OMERE (TRAD)**.

The project simulated trapped electron and proton fluxes using **AE8/AP8 models** and analyzed solar particle events to characterize the radiation environment. Compared radiation exposure between a **sun-synchronous LEO (800 km)** and a **Galileo MEO** orbit, and performed **cumulative dose and sectoring analyses using SHIELDOSE-2** to assess the survival of a 100 krad-rated SRAM under 5 mmAl shielding.
