Designed and implemented a **modular three-layer skill-based architecture** for autonomous robotic systems within a **ROS 2** workspace.

Structured the robot's planning domain and problem instances using **PDDL**, with a **Python wrapper** interfacing with the **Fast Forward Planner** to enable flexible and intelligent autonomous decision-making in a **Gazebo** simulation environment.
