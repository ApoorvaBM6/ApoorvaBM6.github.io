Designed and implemented a **smart vehicle prototype incorporating automotive black box (Event Data Recorder) technology** to record vehicle parameters and analyze events leading to accidents.

Developed a sensor-based embedded system integrating **collision, temperature, alcohol, door, and ultrasonic sensors** with a microcontroller, EEPROM, LCD, GSM module, and motor driver. The system monitored vehicle conditions during operation, stored parameters before and during collisions, and used **GSM communication to send accident notifications** to a predefined contact.

Implemented safety logic to allow vehicle motion only when the **door was closed and alcohol levels were below a defined threshold**, while collision sensors detected impacts and the ultrasonic sensor provided vehicle-speed information before a collision.
