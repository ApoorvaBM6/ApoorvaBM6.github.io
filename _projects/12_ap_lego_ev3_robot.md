Designed and implemented a **reactive controller** for autonomous navigation and obstacle detection using a **LEGO Mindstorms EV3** robot and the **Lustre synchronous dataflow language**.

The project integrated **bumper, ultrasonic, and color sensors** for environmental perception, with **two-wheel motor control** for forward/backward movement and left/right turns. Developed sensor-driven state transitions for navigation, obstacle handling, and directional changes, demonstrating deterministic and modular reactive control.
