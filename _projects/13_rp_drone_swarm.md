Conducted a structured literature survey of interaction techniques for **drone and robot swarm teleoperation**, with a particular focus on Human–Swarm Interaction (HSI) and operator cognitive workload.

The survey synthesized current Human–Swarm Interaction research into a framework connecting **interaction techniques, visualization, autonomy, multimodal feedback and cognitive workload**.

The resulting design principles emphasize **abstraction-based visualization, adaptive autonomy, predictive mission support and multimodal feedback** as key components for scalable, safe and cognitively efficient teleoperation of large autonomous swarms.
