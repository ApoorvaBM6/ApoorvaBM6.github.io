Analyzed the **communication performance and schedulability** of a **MIL-STD-1553B avionics network** based on a representative Rafale aircraft architecture.

Developed a **Python performance analysis tool** to parse message specifications and calculate transmission, access, and end-to-end delays, incorporating message size, transmission rate, and protocol overhead. Applied **non-preemptive Rate Monotonic scheduling** to evaluate message schedulability and real-time network performance.
