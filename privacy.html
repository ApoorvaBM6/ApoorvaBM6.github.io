Privacy notice

With your consent, this website uses Umami Analytics (hosted by Umami Software in the EU) to produce aggregate visitor statistics: how many people visit, which sections and projects they look at, how they found the site, and whether documents such as the CV are downloaded. Nothing is measured unless you click “Accept” in the banner shown on your first visit.

What is collected

  • Pages and sections viewed, approximate time spent, and clicks on links and buttons of this site (for example “CV downloaded — English”).
  • The referring website (for example LinkedIn or Google), campaign tags in the link, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language.
  • Country, region and city, derived from your IP address at the moment of the visit. Your IP address is not stored.

What is not collected

  • No analytics cookies. The only item stored on your device is your consent choice itself (see below).
  • No names, e-mail addresses, form contents or other personal identifiers; no device fingerprinting; no tracking across other websites.
  • No advertising, profiling or sale of data. Statistics are only viewed by the site owner, in aggregate form.

Purpose, legal basis and retention

The sole purpose is to measure the audience of this personal portfolio. Statistics are only collected on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, art. 82 of the French Data Protection Act), which you can withdraw at any time — as easily as you gave it — using the buttons below. Statistics are kept for at most 6 months by the analytics provider.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are treated as a refusal: if your browser sends either, you will not be asked and no statistics are recorded.

Your consent choice

Your answer is stored in your browser (local storage key analytics-consent) for 6 months so that you are not asked on every visit; after that you are asked again. This storage is strictly necessary to remember your choice.
